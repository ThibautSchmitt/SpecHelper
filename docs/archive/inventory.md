## Superseded descriptions

Earlier reports retain original five-minute limits, sequential-worker observations,
optional verifier/comparison controls, initial categories and manual skill assignments.
They document their tested versions. They do not configure new analyses. Current
rules live only in thematic owners; original dates, failures and measurements are
preserved rather than rewritten into a retrospective claim of success.
